遊戲資料

Still Driving 是單人離線遊戲。你可以直接遊玩，無需建立帳戶或登入。Asympta 不透過遊戲收集個人資料。遊戲沒有廣告或追蹤，也不需要你提供實際位置。

裝置上的存檔

行程、車輛位置和遊戲內收入保存在裝置上的遊戲存檔中。遊戲不會把存檔上傳至 Asympta。你可以從裝置刪除 App 並移除其資料。

電郵支援

如果你主動聯絡，我們使用你提供的地址和訊息處理支援。私隱問題請電郵 hello@everformlab.com。

Privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026. Still Driving is a single-player offline game. You can play without an account or sign-in. Asympta does not collect personal data through the game. The app does not contain advertising or tracking, and does not ask for your real-world location.

Your ride, car position, and in-game earnings are stored in the app's save on your device. The game does not upload those saves to Asympta. You can delete the app and its data using your device settings.

If you email us for support, we use the address and information you provide to respond. For privacy questions, contact hello@everformlab.com.
