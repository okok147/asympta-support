Still Driving

一段安靜的城市車程

遊戲操作私隱政策English

如何開始

  1. 橫向握住裝置。打開遊戲內的手機並發動引擎，等新行程出現後接單。
  2. 放下手機後踩油門，拖動方向盤外圈轉向。鬆開油門會滑行，踩煞車可以停穩。
  3. 在淺色路邊停穩，再用手機確認接客。到第一個路口選擇 App 路線或河邊熟路。
  4. 錯過轉彎時，留意下一個路口的指示。如果駛過停靠點，請停穩、點 D／R 換倒檔，再慢慢退回。
  5. 到診所側門停穩後，用手機確認抵達。重開遊戲會保留行程並從靜止開始。

Support

Hold the device in landscape. Open the in-game phone to start the engine and accept the ride. Put the phone down, drag the outer rim to steer, and hold the accelerator or brake. Stop at the marked curb and confirm pickup or arrival on the phone.

Use the next junction to recover a missed turn. If you pass a curb, stop and tap D/R to reverse. The game saves your ride and resumes with the car stopped.

For help, email hello@everformlab.com with your device model, system version, and the steps that led to the issue. Privacy policy
