Plans and pricing

On iPhone and iPad, you can continue using the app after the free-use allowance with an active monthly or annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. The monthly and annual plans renew automatically. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase with no renewal. Apple shows the local price and billing period before you confirm.

Trial, renewal, and cancellation

Eligible Apple Accounts can start a seven-day free trial on a subscription plan. Apple determines eligibility and displays the trial and renewal terms before purchase. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current term ends to avoid the next charge. Buying lifetime access does not cancel an existing subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions with Apple.

Restore purchases or ask for help

Use Subscription → Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used to purchase. For billing or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support. For questions about the app, contact Thinking support.
