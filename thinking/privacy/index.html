What the app uses

Documents and settings

Asympta Thinking stores the text, titles, document relationships, editing marks, settings, and free-use counters it needs on your device. When iCloud Drive is available and enabled for the app, it uses your private iCloud app storage to keep documents in sync. Asympta operates no separate server for your documents. Files you export are saved or shared to the destination you choose.

Purchases and access

Apple processes App Store payments. The app reads verified StoreKit transactions, such as the product and entitlement status, to manage subscriptions and lifetime access. Apple handles payment details. The app may check your app-specific iCloud identifier to determine access eligibility and cache that result securely on the device.

Support and website

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. If you email support, we use your address and what you send to answer and follow up. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations. GitHub serves this website and Apple handles iCloud and App Store data under their privacy policies.

Apple Privacy Policy · GitHub Privacy Statement

Manage or delete your data

You can remove documents in the app. Local recovery copies and exported files may remain until you delete them separately. To stop future iCloud sync, disable iCloud for the app in system settings. Manage remaining iCloud copies and backups from your Apple Account. To request deletion of data you sent to support, email hello@everformlab.com.

文件與設定

Asympta Thinking 將文字、標題、文件關係、編輯標記、設定與免費使用計數儲存在你的裝置上。當裝置可用 iCloud 雲碟，且已為 App 開啟 iCloud 時，App 使用你專用的 iCloud 儲存空間同步文件。Asympta 沒有營運獨立伺服器來儲存你的文件。匯出的檔案會儲存或分享至你指定的位置。

購買與使用權益

App Store 付款由 Apple 處理。App 讀取經 StoreKit 驗證的交易資料，例如商品和權益狀態，以管理訂閱及終身使用權。付款資料由 Apple 處理。App 可能查詢僅適用於本 App 的 iCloud 識別碼，以確認使用資格，並將結果安全地暫存在裝置上。

支援與網站

App 未內嵌第三方廣告或分析 SDK。若你以電郵聯絡支援，我們會使用你的電郵地址和來信內容回覆及跟進，並按處理需求或法律義務保留往來紀錄。本網站由 GitHub 提供託管，iCloud 與 App Store 資料由 Apple 依各自的隱私政策處理。
