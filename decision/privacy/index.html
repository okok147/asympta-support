Asympta Decision - Privacy

Updated 1 November 2026. Applies to Asympta Decision for iPhone and iPad, formerly Life Decision - Yes or No?

On your device

We do not collect your questions or answers. A question stays in app memory and is not sent to us or an AI service. Preferences and diagnostic files are stored locally. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics service or tracking SDK.

Optional recordings

When you choose to record and allow the system prompt, the app saves video locally. Camera and microphone capture are disabled. A recording may include a question visible on screen. The app does not upload recordings. Sharing happens only when you choose a destination through the system share sheet; the destination's privacy policy applies.

Apple services and external links

Apple processes optional support purchases. We do not receive payment card details. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight and device diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy. External credit and support links open services governed by their own policies.

Control and contact

Deleting the app removes its app-local files; exported copies and backups are managed separately. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Avoid including private questions or unnecessary personal information.
