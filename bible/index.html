Asympta Bible Support

Reading and listening

The English and Traditional Chinese Bible editions are included for offline reading. In the reader, open Menu to change the interface language and Scripture edition separately. Under Listen, choose a voice, preview it, then select Play from Here. While reading aloud, you can pause or resume in Control Center or on the Lock Screen.

Home Screen widget

Add Asympta Bible from the Home Screen widget gallery. Tap the displayed verse to open it in the reader. Set the rotation interval in Menu under Widget. The default is 15 minutes, and iOS determines when refreshes occur.

Personal library

Export a backup from the personal library before moving devices. Use the import option to merge a saved backup.

閱讀與朗讀

英文和繁體中文聖經皆可離線閱讀。在閱讀頁打開「選單」，可分別切換介面語言和經文版本。在朗讀區選擇並試聽聲音，再點「從這裡播放」。朗讀期間可從控制中心或鎖定畫面暫停與續播。

主畫面小工具

從主畫面的小工具圖庫加入 Asympta Bible。點選顯示的經節，即可在閱讀器開啟。在「選單」的小工具區可調整輪替間隔。預設為 15 分鐘，實際更新由 iOS 排程。

私人藏書

換裝置前，可從私人藏書匯出備份，再用匯入功能合併備份。
