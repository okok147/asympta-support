Asympta Bible Support

Reading and listening

The English and Traditional Chinese Bible editions are included for offline reading. In the reader, open Settings → Reading to change the interface language and Scripture edition separately. Open Settings → Listen and tap Play from Here, or Continue Listening to resume. Open Voice to preview a voice, switch between Mandarin and Cantonese for Chinese Scripture, or choose the app default, iOS default or another installed voice. While reading aloud, you can pause or resume in Control Center or on the Lock Screen.

Home Screen widget

Add Asympta Bible from the Home Screen widget gallery. Tap the displayed verse to open it in the reader. Set the rotation interval under Settings → Home Screen Widget. The default is 15 minutes, and iOS determines when refreshes occur.

Asympta Plus

Open Settings → Asympta Plus subscription to compare plans. The annual plan is billed once a year and shows a monthly equivalent for comparison. If you already subscribed, use Restore purchases with the Apple Account you used to subscribe, or Manage subscription to change or cancel your plan.

Personal library

Before moving devices, use Settings → Backup and restore to export a file. To bring it to your new device, open the same screen and merge the saved backup. You can save a copy in iCloud Drive.

閱讀與朗讀

英文和繁體中文聖經皆可離線閱讀。在閱讀頁打開「設定」→「閱讀」，可分別切換介面語言和經文版本。從「設定」→「朗讀」可從目前經節開始，或接續上次朗讀。打開「聲音」即可試聽。繁體中文經文可選國語或粵語，也可以選 App 預設、iOS 預設或其他已安裝的聲音。朗讀期間可從控制中心或鎖定畫面暫停與續播。

主畫面小工具

從主畫面的小工具圖庫加入 Asympta Bible。點選顯示的經節，即可在閱讀器開啟。在「設定」→「主畫面小工具」可調整輪替間隔。預設為 15 分鐘，實際更新由 iOS 排程。

Asympta Plus 訂閱

打開「設定」→「Asympta Plus 訂閱」比較方案。年訂閱每年扣款一次，平均月費供比較。已訂閱者可使用原本購買的 Apple 帳號「回復購買」，也可以從「管理訂閱」變更或取消方案。

私人藏書

換裝置前，可從「設定」→「備份與還原」匯出檔案，再於新裝置從同一頁合併備份。你可以把副本存到 iCloud 雲碟。
