Asympta Bible Privacy Policy

Asympta Bible requires no app account. We do not collect personal data through the app. The app keeps your notes, handwriting, bookmarks, reading history and settings on your device. The widget uses a shared container on your device to access its display content and preferences.

Narration uses Apple’s voices on your device. Optional reading reminders use local notifications. If you choose to export a note, image or library backup, the copy goes to the destination you select. A backup saved in iCloud Drive is stored in your iCloud account. You can remove your local library by deleting the app and manage exported copies in their destinations.

If you email us for help, we use the information you send to answer your request.

隱私權政策

Asympta Bible 無須建立 App 帳戶。我們不透過 App 蒐集個人資料。筆記、手寫內容、書籤、閱讀紀錄及設定保存在你的裝置上。小工具從同一部裝置的共享容器讀取顯示內容及偏好設定。

朗讀使用裝置上的 Apple 語音。選用的讀經提醒使用本機通知。若匯出筆記、圖片或藏書備份，副本會存往你選擇的位置。存入 iCloud 雲碟的備份屬於你的 iCloud 帳戶。刪除 App 可移除裝置上的私人藏書，已匯出的副本可在其儲存位置管理。

如果你以電郵聯絡我們，我們會使用你提供的資訊來回覆你的支援請求。
