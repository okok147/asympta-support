Support

Organize your Journey branches

Open a habit, choose Edit habit, then open Journey category to choose a branch keyword. Past entries follow the habit into its new branch and the habit keeps its ink color.

Set your language

Choose English or Traditional Chinese when you first open the journal. Switch later in Settings → Language.

Back up your journal

Use Settings → Export JSON backup to make a restorable copy. To load it, choose Restore JSON backup and confirm. Restoring replaces the current journal. Export a copy of your current journal first if you wish to keep it.

Capture a note

In a habit record or a goal progress entry, tap the pencil icon on an iPad to write using Apple Pencil and Scribble. Habit Plus adds an in-app microphone button that turns speech into editable text using supported on-device speech recognition. Review the note before saving the entry.

Restore or manage Habit Plus

Open Settings → Habit Plus → Restore purchases using the Apple Account you subscribed with. Manage or cancel your Apple subscription.

Contact

Email hello@everformlab.com for help. Include your app version and what happened. Remove private journal details from any attachments.

使用支援

整理旅程分枝

開啟習慣，點「編輯習慣」，在「旅程分類」設定分枝關鍵字。移動分枝時，歷史紀錄會跟著該習慣移動，習慣的墨色也會保留。

切換語言

首次開啟日誌時可選擇繁體中文或英文。之後可到「設定」的「語言」切換。

備份日誌

在「設定」點「匯出 JSON 備份」，即可建立可回復的副本。選擇「還原 JSON 備份」並確認後，備份會取代目前的日誌。若要保留目前的日誌，請先另存一份備份。

記下做過的事

在習慣紀錄或目標進度中，可於 iPad 點筆尖圖示，用 Apple Pencil 和隨手寫輸入。Habit Plus 提供 App 內的麥克風按鈕，可在支援的裝置上將語音轉為文字。儲存前可以檢查和修改。

恢復或管理 Habit Plus

使用原本訂閱的 Apple 帳號，在「設定」的 Habit Plus 頁面點「恢復購買」。也可以管理或取消 Apple 訂閱。
