Habit Plus

Habit Plus unlocks in-app dictation on devices and in languages that support on-device speech recognition. The journal and your saved notes stay available without a subscription.

Habit Plus has two auto-renewing plans. The U.S. prices are US$9.99 billed monthly or US$99.99 billed annually. The annual plan is about US$8.33 per month, billed as one annual payment. Apple shows the price for your storefront before you confirm.

Eligible Apple Accounts can start a seven-day free trial with either plan. Apple determines eligibility across the subscription group. After the trial, Apple charges the selected plan price unless you cancel. To avoid renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current subscription period ends.

Manage or cancel with Apple. You can restore a purchase from Settings → Habit Plus.

Asympta Habit uses the Apple Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Habit Plus 訂閱條款

Habit Plus 提供 App 內的語音輸入，適用於支援裝置端語音辨識的裝置及語言。即使沒有訂閱，你仍可使用日誌及已儲存的備註。

Habit Plus 提供兩種自動續訂方案。美國商店月繳為每月 US$9.99，年繳為每年 US$99.99，一次扣款，約等於每月 US$8.33。確認購買前，Apple 會顯示你所在商店的價格。

符合資格的 Apple 帳號可選任一方案，享 7 天免費試用。資格由 Apple 按訂閱群組判定。試用結束後，Apple 會按選定方案收費。若要避免續訂，請在試用或目前訂閱期結束至少 24 小時前取消。
