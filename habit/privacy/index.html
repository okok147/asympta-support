Journal and backups

Your habits, goals, notes and preferences are stored on your device. Asympta Habit has no Asympta account, ads, analytics SDK or tracking. Asympta does not receive your journal. Exporting sends a copy to the Files location or destination you select. A device or cloud backup may include app data according to your Apple settings. You can remove the in-app journal using Settings → Delete all journal data. Exported copies are managed separately in the location where you saved them.

Handwriting and voice

Handwriting uses Apple's Scribble on iPad. If you choose to start in-app dictation, the app requests microphone and speech recognition permission. Dictation uses on-device recognition for supported languages. Asympta Habit does not retain or upload audio. Review the converted text and choose whether to add it to a note. You can revoke microphone and speech recognition permissions in your device settings.

Purchases

Apple handles payment, subscription renewals and purchase restoration. The app reads StoreKit's verified entitlement to enable Habit Plus and does not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your Apple Account and payments is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Contact

If you contact us, we use the address and information you provide to respond to and resolve your request. For support or privacy questions, email hello@everformlab.com.

日誌與備份

習慣、目標、備註和偏好設定儲存在你的裝置上。Asympta Habit 無須建立 Asympta 帳號，沒有廣告、分析 SDK 或追蹤。Asympta 不會收到你的日誌。匯出時，副本會存到你選取的「檔案」位置或其他目的地。裝置或雲端備份是否包含 App 資料，取決於你的 Apple 設定。你可在 App「設定」選擇「刪除所有日誌資料」。已匯出的副本須在各自儲存位置管理。

手寫與語音

手寫使用 iPad 的 Apple 隨手寫。你選擇在 App 開始語音輸入時，App 會請求麥克風和語音辨識權限。支援的語言會在裝置上辨識。Asympta Habit 不保留或上傳音訊。你可以檢查辨識出的文字，並選擇是否加入備註。可在裝置設定撤回相關權限。

購買

Apple 處理付款、續訂和恢復購買。App 讀取 StoreKit 已驗證的購買權益來啟用 Habit Plus，不會取得你的付款資料。Apple 如何處理帳號和付款資料，請參閱Apple 隱私政策。

聯絡

如你主動聯絡，我們會使用你提供的電郵地址和資料回覆並處理請求。支援或隱私問題請電郵 hello@everformlab.com。
