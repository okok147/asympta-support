經文、錄音與本機設定

App 不需建立帳戶。匯入的經文與錄音、播放位置、完成遍數及背景播放設定都留在你的裝置。使用或準備這些功能，以及在付款前草擬留言，App 都不會連線至隨喜留言服務。App 不使用廣告、分析 SDK 或跨網站追蹤。你主動開啟外部網頁時，該網站的主機可能為營運和安全處理一般連線資料。

Apple 訂閱、隨喜留言與共修

Apple 處理扣款及退款；Asympta 不會收到你的卡號或 Apple 帳戶資料。Apple 確認訂閱後，App 才將 Apple 簽署的交易憑證和隨機作者識別碼傳給 Cloudflare。留言內容及類別只在你選擇送出時上傳。若你啟用 iCloud 鑰匙圈，作者識別資料可透過 Apple 在你的裝置間同步。服務用這些資料核對付款、避免重複投稿、處理退款、審核留言和保護留言板。服務會用來源 IP 在 Cloudflare 邊緣限制請求頻率；留言資料庫不保存 IP 或完整的 Apple 簽署憑證。

訂閱者可看見通過自動安全檢查的留言文字、類別及刊登日；App 使用隨機作者代號讓讀者可以隱藏作者。審核者可查看待審稿件、審核紀錄和檢舉。請勿在留言放入姓名、聯絡方式或其他可識別資料。留言、交易查核紀錄與審核資料一般在送達後一年內依排程移除；Apple 另行保留自己的付款紀錄。你可以提早刪除留言正文。

加入線上共修時，Cloudflare 短期保存隨機作者代號與共修到期時間，供計算共修人數。其他參與者只會看見總人數。你離開後即停止計數，裝置離線時，紀錄會在預定的 15 分鐘刷新時間後保留最多 90 秒。

要查閱或刪除資料，請到資料管理與刪除。你以電郵聯絡我們時，我們會使用你提供的資料回覆和處理請求。

Privacy Policy

No app account is required. Imported scripture and audio, playback progress and settings stay on your device. The app does not contact the board while you listen or draft a comment before paying. We do not use ads, analytics SDKs or cross-site tracking. When you choose to open an external page, its host may process standard connection data for operation and security.

Apple handles billing and refunds. We do not receive your payment card or Apple Account details. After Apple confirms an active subscription, Cloudflare processes signed Apple proof and a random author identifier. The app sends your comment and its category when you submit it. If iCloud Keychain is enabled, Apple can sync the author credentials across your devices. We use this information to verify the payment, stop duplicate submissions, moderate the board and handle refunds. Cloudflare uses your IP address to limit requests at the edge; the board database does not store the address or the full signed Apple proof.

Subscribers can see text that passed the automated safety checks, its category and the publication date. A random author ID lets readers hide an author. Moderators can see submissions, moderation records and reports. Avoid including personal or contact information. The board generally removes submissions, verification records and moderation data on a schedule after one year. You can delete your comment text sooner. Apple keeps its own payment records.

If you join online group practice, Cloudflare temporarily stores the random author identifier and the presence expiration time to tally participants. Other participants only see the total. Leaving stops your participation, and an offline session expires at most 90 seconds after its scheduled 15-minute refresh.

See Data and deletion for your options. If you email us, we use the information you send to handle your request.
