資料管理與刪除

English

本機經文、錄音及播放紀錄

在「檔案 → 我的 iPhone／iPad → Asympta Dharani → DharaniPreview」管理匯入的檔案。到 App 的「設定 → 播放 → 重新開始」可清除本次播放進度。刪除 App 前，請先將想保留的檔案複製到其他位置。

隨喜留言

在訂閱裝置或使用相同 iCloud 鑰匙圈的裝置開啟「設定 → 隨喜 → 我的留言」，可查閱送達及審核狀態，也可選擇「刪除留言」。刪除會清除正文，並將已刊登的留言從留言板移除。最少量的付款與審核紀錄會限時保留，以防止同一交易重複用來投稿及協助處理退款。

如果遺失裝置、希望取得資料副本或要求刪除其他留言服務資料，請聯絡 Asympta 支援。提供請求類型與可用的交易日期即可開始；請勿寄送密碼、付款卡資料或完整交易憑證。可能需要核對交易才能處理。

可在 Apple 訂閱設定管理或取消月訂閱。離開共修可退出人數統計。刪除留言不會自動退款。扣款或退款請到 Apple 問題回報。

Data and Deletion

Manage imported files in Files → On My iPhone/iPad → Asympta Dharani → DharaniPreview. To clear the current playback session, choose Restart in Settings. Copy any files you want to keep before deleting the app.

On the device you subscribed with or a device with your synced iCloud Keychain, open Settings → Support → My Comments to check your submission or delete it. Deletion clears the text and removes it from the board if it was published. Limited transaction and moderation records are retained temporarily to prevent reuse and handle refunds.

If you have lost access to the device, want a copy of your data or want to request further deletion, email Asympta support. Start with the type of request and the purchase date if available. Do not email passwords, payment card details or the full signed proof. We may need to verify the purchase before acting.

Use Apple’s subscription settings to manage or cancel your monthly subscription. You can leave practice to stop appearing in the participant count. Deleting a comment does not issue a refund. For billing and refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem.
