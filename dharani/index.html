如何取得協助

播放問題請先確認經文和錄音仍在「檔案 → 我的 iPhone／iPad → Asympta Dharani → DharaniPreview」。可在「設定 → 播放」調整背景播放或重新開始。

已付款但無法留言或加入共修時，請用購買時的 Apple 帳號，在「設定 → 隨喜訂閱」選擇「恢復購買」。留言的送達與審核狀態可在「設定 → 留言 → 我的留言」查看。

App 使用問題請電郵聯絡 Asympta 支援。請附上 App 版本、裝置型號及問題發生的步驟。扣款及退款請使用 Apple 問題回報。

Support

If playback is unavailable, check that your scripture and audio are in Files → On My iPhone/iPad → Asympta Dharani → DharaniPreview. Open Settings → Playback to change background playback or restart.

If you have an active subscription and can't post or join group practice, use the Apple Account you purchased with and choose Restore Purchases from Settings → Subscription. Check the status of a comment in Settings → Comments → My Comments.

For help using the app, email Asympta Support with your app version, device model, and the steps that led to the issue. For billing and refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem.
