Asympta Reminder Support

Write or speak something to remember. Tap a Thing’s words to open its details, or tap its outline to mark it done.

Reminders and widgets

A subscription activates new timed reminders. Check the Thing’s planned time and reminder time, then check notification permissions and Focus in iOS Settings.

To add the widget, touch and hold the Home Screen, choose Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Asympta Reminder. Tap a visible Thing in the widget to open it.

Subscriptions

Open Settings in Asympta Reminder to view plans, restore purchases, or manage your subscription with Apple. Existing Things stay on your device after a subscription ends.
